IT SUPPORT POLICY

Password Reset:
Employees can reset their passwords through the company self-service portal.

VPN Access:
Employees working remotely must connect to the company VPN to access internal systems.

IT Support:
Employees can contact the IT help desk for technical issues.

Account Lockout:
Employees should contact the IT help desk if their account is locked.
VPN Connection:
1. Open the company VPN application.
2. Enter your company credentials.
3. Select the company VPN server.
4. Click Connect.
5. Contact the IT help desk if the connection fails.
