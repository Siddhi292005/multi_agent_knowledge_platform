EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Leave Application:
Employees must submit leave requests through the employee portal.

Manager Approval:
Leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days requires manager approval.

Carry Forward:
Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the following calendar year.
